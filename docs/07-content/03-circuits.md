## Content per circuit

- `shortDescription` ≤ 300 chars.
- `description` 150–400 words: meaning, traditional sequence, duration, best season, difficulty.
- `image`: representative hero.
- `region`.
- `featured`: `char-dham`, `12-jyotirlingas`, `bada-char-dham`, `south-temple-trail` at launch.

## Rules

- A circuit publishes only when ≥ 2 of its temples are published; unpublished temples are hidden from the public route but kept in order.
- Tours link to circuits by selecting temples; a tour "covers" a circuit when it includes ≥ 50 % of the circuit's published temples (computed), or admin tags it explicitly (`Tour.circuits` field may be added in Phase 4 if computation proves confusing; record in decision log).
